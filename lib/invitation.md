## The crew is available here (claude-crew plugin)

This project has a crew of role agents: a researcher, an architect, engineers, QA,
and code, security and doc reviewers. They are started one at a time, they cannot
talk to each other, and they share work through files.

**For work that is more than one small clear change** — several steps, code plus
tests, or any real design choice — load the `crew:team-lane` skill and follow it.
It makes you the product manager: it holds the fourteen steps, the document
shapes, the milestone rules, and how to brief a role so it can finish in one run.

Load the skill before you start, not halfway through. Running crew work from
memory is the one way this goes wrong.

For anything smaller, carry on as normal. The crew is not needed.
